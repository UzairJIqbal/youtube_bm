Privacy Policy — YouTube Study Bookmarks

Last updated: 24 October 2026

YouTube Study Bookmarks is a browser extension for saving timestamped bookmarks on YouTube videos. This policy explains what it stores and where.

What the extension stores

When you save a bookmark, the extension stores the following in your own browser only:

  • the YouTube video's ID and title
  • the time of the bookmark or clip
  • any title, note or tags you add
  • your settings (for example the automatic rewind)

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else. There is no server.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not require an account.
  • It does not read or store your browsing history. It runs only on www.youtube.com.

Your control over your data

  • You can export your bookmarks as a Markdown or backup file from the Settings page. Files you export are saved on your own device.
  • Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data from your browser.

Links you choose to open

The "Join the waitlist" button opens a separate web form. Anything you enter there is covered by that form provider's privacy policy, not by the extension.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your device, such as optional sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and those features will be opt-in.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/UzairJIqbal/youtube_bm/issues
